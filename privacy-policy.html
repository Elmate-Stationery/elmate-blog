Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

What we collect

Marginalia is a static site with no database. We do not collect personal information beyond what you voluntarily submit through the newsletter or contact form (name and email address), and standard, non-identifying analytics such as page views, if analytics is enabled in data/config.js.

How we use it

Email addresses submitted through the newsletter form are used only to send new-post notifications. Contact form submissions are used only to respond to your message. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

Cookies & local storage

This site stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage — your theme preference, bookmarked articles, and recently viewed articles. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Third-party services

If enabled, this site may use Google Analytics, Plausible Analytics, Formspree, Mailchimp, or Giscus (GitHub Discussions) for comments. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing data they collect directly.

Your rights

You may request removal of any information you've submitted by contacting us at the email address on the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date above.
