8. Create an Office Supply Budget

A simple stationery budget can prevent unnecessary spending.

Start by identifying the products your office uses every month. Then divide them into three groups:

Essential:
Products that are used every day.

Regular:
Products that are purchased occasionally.

Optional:
Products that are useful but not necessary.

This makes it easier to prioritize spending and avoid buying unnecessary stationery.
